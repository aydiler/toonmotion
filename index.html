ToonMotion

ToonMotion publishes animated, narrated YouTube Shorts of manhwa/webtoon chapters.

The ToonMotion tools sign in to YouTube with Google OAuth only to manage the operator's own ToonMotion channels: uploading the operator's videos, updating channel branding, and reading view, subscriber and analytics statistics for an internal dashboard. They do not access anyone else's account or data.
